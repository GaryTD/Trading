Risk Layer · base size · stop · 1R cap new in v2
Base Risk / R
0.75% / full position
Your dial. R = max account loss on a fully-built (3/3) position. Set it once; everything below sizes off it. Conservative 0.5%, standard 0.75–1%.
Tranche Size
⅓ · ⅓ · ⅓
Each rung is one third of the full intended position. Tranche-1 size = (its share of R) ÷ initial-stop distance. Never front-load; the ladder earns the next third.
Initial Stop · T1
Structure first
Long: below the green-star signal-bar low / lower band, whichever is cleaner. Short: mirror above. ATR is a filter, not the placement: if structure is <1×ATR(14) away → entry's inside the noise, skip/wait. Fallback only when structure is ambiguous: ~2×ATR(14).
Aggregate Open Risk
≤ 1R
Hard ceiling. Total live risk across all tranches never exceeds 1R. Adds are funded by moving the prior tranche to breakeven — not by adding new risk.

v2 changelog & open flags
  1. Green star now marks long entry (was pink). Red star = short/exit at highs.
  2. Risk layer added: base R, ⅓ tranche sizing off stop distance, structural initial stop with ATR alt, and the ≤1R aggregate cap.
  3. Breakeven-before-add enforced: no tranche 2 until T1 is at BE, no tranche 3 until T2 is at BE. This is what stops the pyramid from leaving you largest at the worst average on a reversal.
  4. Regime gate promoted from context to a first-check on both sides. On the current tape it blocks new shorts (cycle reads Accumulation) — counter-cycle adds are manage-only.
  5. Still your call: the 0.75% R and ATR multiples (1× filter / 2× fallback) are placeholders — set to your own tolerance.
  6. v2.1 fixes: short scale-out is now thirds (first trim −⅓, not −½); final third on both sides is a discretionary total exit — black bar / MQ recross (short) and orange-mid / red star (long) are the cues, you pull the trigger. ATR demoted from placement to filter (1× min-distance) + fallback (2×).
  7. v2.2: BTC ⇄ Equities selector on the ATR Stop Profile. The rule is identical — ATR self-normalizes — so the selector only swaps the magnitudes (1× filter / 2× fallback in %), the gap/session caveats ATR can't see, and which regime inputs apply.

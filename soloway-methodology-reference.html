01

Confirmation Signal

Confirmation Analysis · the filter

The headline proprietary tactic. A trendline break or level break is not a signal by itself — it's a candidate. The Confirmation Signal requires the next candle to close beyond the extreme of the initial break candle before the break is tradeable. Designed to filter false breaks, fakeouts and bear traps.

Rule (down)
Daily close below the low of the initial breakdown candle confirms the break.
Rule (up)
Daily close above the high of the initial breakout candle confirms the break.
Worked ex.
QQQ channel break (Nov '25): the break alone is "one thing" — confirmation was a daily close below 606, the low of the breakdown candle, to open the path toward the correction target.
Trades
Acceptance, not the break. Pairs with "no retest = no trade," break-and-back retest, and a negation/engulfing candle closing back inside range.
Trade-off
Lagging by design — you surrender the exact turn in exchange for higher hit-rate. Siblings in the PPT course: Three Tail Theory, River Theory (detail paywalled).
↳ Hooks into your stack This is the discretionary twin of your gate-based confirmation logic (Track D). A tactical oscillator firing counter-trend (Keltner, PowerX) is the "break"; it isn't actionable until the regime gates confirm — exactly the "all three gates closed" constraint. Soloway formalises the same instinct as a single close-beyond-the-candle test.

Cycle Analysis

Cyclical Analysis · the regime read

The asymmetry thesis: major tops are processes, major bottoms are events. Tops grind and roll over as "buy-the-dip" conditioning gets exhausted in stages; the true V-bottom capitulation happens at the lows, after the dip-buy has failed repeatedly — not at the highs.

Channel read
Rejection from the top of a well-defined multi-year parallel channel = trend exhaustion. (S&P weekly channel anchored off the 2021 peak; its lower rail had marked five prior bottoms.)
Extension
Historic over-extension (e.g. SMH) flags drawdown risk before price confirms it.
Sentiment
Extreme negative sentiment = bullish tell; euphoric "you can't lose" = his cue to step back.
Targets
Former major highs become future support — the first logical magnet for a corrective move.
Analog
2008: down → bounce → lower → bounce → terminal capitulation months after the top. The panic isn't at the high.
↳ Hooks into your stack A discretionary cross-check on your Hybrid Rotation Engine phase reads and the DCAS P6/P7 desync. Soloway's "capitulation lives at the lows" maps cleanly onto late-distribution → early-accumulation: a P6/P7 label sitting on top of a deep price decline is the exact "top as process" picture, and supports favouring the better-calibrated classifier.

Time Layer

Time Chart · pairing price with a window

Note: unlike the Confirmation Signal, this isn't a single named proprietary tool — it's the timing discipline woven through the method. The signature habit is issuing price targets bound to explicit time windows, and structuring trade horizons so a thesis has room to play out.

Price + time
Targets come with a clock: "10% S&P / 15% Nasdaq over the next 1–2 months"; gold "$5,000 by the first month or two of 2026."
Horizon
Mid-term book runs 3–12 months; the options service deliberately uses ~6-month expiries so theta doesn't kill a correct-but-early thesis.
Multi-TF
Levels must be obvious on the daily / 4H without squinting; drop a timeframe to time the entry once the higher TF level is in play.
Patience
Monitor time-in-trade / time-to-profit; cut slow movers; pause any setup that fails three times in a row.
↳ Hooks into your stack This is your M2 lag-window logic in discretionary form — a constructive read can hold even while the tape is bearish because the thesis carries a time horizon, not just a level. His ~6-month expiry preference is the direct argument for the IBIT LEAPS side of your proxy fork: leverage with enough runway for the BTC regime call to resolve.
04

VWAP / Anchored VWAP

VWAP Method · the institutional value line

At Verified Investing the dedicated VWAP course is run by "Doctor B" in collaboration with Soloway ("Mastering VWAP"). The swing-relevant core is Anchored VWAP (AVWAP) — VWAP recalculated from a chosen meaningful origin rather than the daily session open, read as the cumulative cost basis of everyone positioned since that event.

Anchor at
Pivotal swing highs/lows, breakout candles, or macro catalysts (CPI, FOMC, earnings). Anchor choice is the analysis — a random anchor gives a random line.
Control
Price above AVWAP = buyers in control; below = sellers. Slope matters — up / down / flat tells you trend strength.
SD bands
1st band = mean-reversion zone; 2nd–3rd = over-extension / "exhaustion zone," prone to a violent snap-back.
The Pinch
When a trendline and an AVWAP converge, a breakout/rejection is imminent — a high-probability decision point.
Confluence
Stack multiple anchors (trend origin + swing high + event); where they cluster, the level firms up.
↳ Hooks into your stack The accumulation-native tool here. Anchor an AVWAP to the BTC cycle low / halving to read the institutional cost basis line — a direct, objective input to your MSTR-shares vs IBIT-LEAPS proxy decision and to dollar-cost timing. AVWAP touch + a confirming candle (Pillar 01) is the cleanest entry trigger your DCAS engine could borrow.

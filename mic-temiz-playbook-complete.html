The
Playbook.

The whole thing in one line: find emotionally overextended small-cap pumps, read the regime, vet the stock, wait for backside exhaustion in your one window, enter with tightly defined risk, take the money, and compound — because you don't make money by making money; you make it by controlling losses. The holy grail was never a setup. It's risk management and process. Price action is king. Assume every stock is trying to trap you.

02

The Universe — Be a Little Fish

What we trade / avoid

Trade This

  • Under $300M cap, under $10/share. Micro ($50–300M), nano (<$50M), penny.
  • Momo names — emotional, volatile, fundamentals out the window. That's the edge.
  • Catalysts that matter: earnings/calls, index additions, genuine PR.

Avoid This

  • Financials, energy, real estate — where the big players hang out.
  • M&A — no idea what happens. Big-cap PR (Amazon/Walmart-tier).
  • 90% of small-cap PR is noise. Don't believe it until proven. Forward-looking events are dangerous.

04

Vetoes — Red Flags & Don't-Shorts

Process · TBradley90

You nail 9 of 10; the 10th sends you to the hole. If a flag shows for a short, best to avoid — but price action is king, don't be blindly biased.

The Big 3 — Squeeze Vetoes

  • Short float >20% — squeeze risk; better as a long.
  • Inst. ownership >40% — they won't let it die.
  • Float <1M — rotation + wide spreads. Avoid early.
  • Others: cap >$100M, PM vol >3M, SSR Day 1, gap <30%, no clear daily S/R.
  • A perfect short is dilutive AND un-squeezable — different checks.

Never Short

  • Frontside Day 1 · micro-floats <3M · reverse splits.
  • 52-wk / all-time-high breakouts with a real catalyst.
  • Pre-market · last 30 min · no-meat stocks.
  • After an "F-U candle" — the reversal that rips through VWAP and traps shorts.
  • Unclear open? Give it 15 min, gauge trend, then attack.

The Pattern Set

MIC · 8 patterns + STUFF
Frontside · Long
First Bounce
  • Price returns to support (line chart) and bounces.
  • PM lines; wait for the wash + bounce. Whole/half-dollar marks — close enough is good enough.
Frontside · Long
VWAP Reclaim
  • Big morning volume that dies, does nothing, traps shorts, then reclaims VWAP. Powerful, often late-day.
  • Low float, SSR a plus, real catalyst, small gap <30%. Shorts' stops at the high get taken — domino.
Frontside · Long
First Green Day
  • Mirror of First Red Day — first decisive green day after a multi-day washout.
  • Longs only; needs strength reclaiming prior structure.
Zombie Time · Long
Zombie Move
  • After 10:30 the dead stock wakes up — trapped shorts who never covered are forced out, squeezing it up.
  • Looks dead at 10:30, longs come in. This is why shorts cut buying power at 10:30.
Short · Transition
First Resistance
  • Brother of First Bounce — first test of resistance is likely to reject. Catch it, sell the pullback, move on.
  • Don't open too close to the line — it needs room to extend. A scalp, not an all-day hold.
Backside · Short · Day-1
Deathline
  • Breakdown of the final support. Day 1 only, small caps only. Emotionally based.
  • PM support → pushes up AM → breaks → fades all day. Support becomes resistance. Small size if unsure.
Backside · Short · A+
First Red Day
  • 100%+ gainer, multiple green days, no big G/R chop. Longs finally have a reason to exit.
  • Best when it gaps up then goes red. Beware red-to-green reclaims. Sell when it breaks VWAP going red.
Backside · Short · Day-2
Low Hanging Fruit
  • Day-1 runner that gaps down Day 2 (after a sell-off), pumping back to the prior day's most-traded price.
  • Trapped longs sell the pump — short the bounce into prior-day resistance. Edge 9:30–10:00. Never long it.

STUFF Move — The Signal

  • A massive rejection — often a multi-/triple-top that can't break and slams; huge wicks. Level 2 sees it first. Support breach unwinds longs and flips the tug-of-war.

09

Execution & The Airbag

Fantasy orders · market stops

Orders & Entries

  • Limit in, limit out. "Fantasy" (pre-planned limit) orders so fear/greed can't move your hand. Fill a few cents early (.02–.04).
  • Stops are MARKET, always. Limit stops don't guarantee a fill — get out at any cost.
  • Need range / meat. No room to fall = no trade. Line-to-line: the lines don't change, your orders do.
  • Add to winners only (offer for shorts, bid for longs). Adding to a loser built a −$450K day.

Exits — Take the Money

  • Pay yourself on the drops. Sell partials into weakness; ~75% off by the end.
  • Cover at lines / support, before bazooka bidders — never arbitrary targets.
  • No runners. Assume every stock will trap you — take it and walk. Time freedom > a few extra dollars.
  • After a red day, just be green tomorrow. +$1 is enough.

Risk & Stops — Non-Negotiable

  • Hard MARKET stop one second after entry. The button after "enter" is always "stop."
  • Broker max daily loss + max shares (~500 learning). Example: $100/trade, $500/day.
  • Risk 1–2% per trade, ~5% max daily. Temiz-style: max loss ≈ 2 days of work. Max size ≈ 1/5 equity.
  • Stop where the thesis dies. Shorts: HoD, prior close, VWAP, higher high, PM high. Longs: LoD, prior close, VWAP, lower low, PM low.

Survive the Curve · Discipline

Stevenrh1

Earn the Size

  • Don't risk capital while learning. Pick one setup, study every video, write the plan.
  • Paper trade (Das Sim) → small shares (good trades, not money — fees are tuition).
  • Size up only after 30–60 days consistent, slowly. Practice the click at 1 share first.
  • Size down + break when a loss stresses you, on a streak, or when stopped out yet right.

Discipline

  • Size down to 50% until a month of consistency. Post the plan (risk/stops, size, entries, covers).
  • WIN = followed the plan; LOSS = deviated — regardless of P&L. Deviate only for a technical reason.
  • Three strikes, you're out. Walk away 5 min after a loss; reset at 4 PM.
  • Guard rails > willpower. Recolor candles, cutoff alarms, silence the phone. Pull profits. Know your numbers.
11

Macro Catalyst Days

FOMC · CPI · Jobs
  • 8:30 prints threaten entries. CPI / NFP / PPI / PCE whip the open; a dovish print rips small-caps and squeezes clean shorts on beta.
  • FOMC (2 PM) threatens holds. Lands after your window — flatten anything carried into 2:00.
  • Don't chase the first 5–15 min — algos overshoot, then fade. Wait for the second move.
  • Macro day = smaller or no new risk. When the whole tape reprices, single-stock edge thins.
Go fishing.
Wait for your plan.
Cast and wait; if the fish doesn't come, you don't jump in and swim after it. Temiz went ten days without a trade, then made his month in one session. Patient to wait, decisive to strike.
